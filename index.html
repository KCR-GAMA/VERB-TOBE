Grammar: Verb "To Be"

We use the verb to be to describe animals and things.

✅ Affirmative (+)

I am a cat. 🐱

It is a dog. 🐶

They are birds. 🐦

❌ Negative (-)

I am not a fish. 🐟

It is not a turtle. 🐢

They are not cats. 🐱

❓ Interrogative (?)

Am I a dog? 🐶

Is it a bird? 🐦

Are they fish? 🐟
